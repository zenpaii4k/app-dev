# My Favorite Series: Saga of Tanya the Evil

> A salaryman is reborn as a young girl in a world at war. She only wants a quiet, stable life, but the war keeps getting in the way.

## Quick Facts

| Detail | Info |
| --- | --- |
| **Title** | Saga of Tanya the Evil (*Youjo Senki*) |
| **Type** | Anime series based on a light novel |
| **Genre** | Military Fantasy / Isekai |
| **Main character** | Tanya Degurechaff |

## Story Summary

**Tanya** was a ruthless salaryman in his past life. He is reborn as a young girl in a world at war, where **Being X** keeps putting her in dangerous events that block her goal of a peaceful life. Instead of giving up, she uses her intelligence and discipline to rise through the military.

## Why I Like It

- **Smart and ruthless main character:** Tanya thinks carefully before every move and always looks for the most efficient way to survive.
- **Exciting war strategies and battles:** The fights are not just about power. Planning, timing, and tactics decide who wins.
- **Magic mixed with modern thinking:** Tanya uses her modern-world mindset, such as business logic and planning, in a world with magic.
- **Rich world-building:** The setting is a different world that borrows elements from World War I and World War II, which makes it feel serious and believable.

## How to Start Watching

1. Watch the anime series.
2. Watch the movie that continues the story.
3. Watch the second season of the series.
4. Read the light novel for more details.
